Why practise before a skills test

Online assessments from platforms such as Indeed, TestGorilla and Kenexa are usually short and timed. Most people who know the material still lose points to the clock or to small slips, such as a missing $ sign or the wrong argument order. Writing the answers yourself a few times before the test removes most of those slips.

How the practice works

Every tool here asks you to do the task, not to pick an option. You get a realistic example, type your answer, and see at once whether it is right and why. Your progress is saved only in your own browser.
