About

Job Test Drills is a free set of practice tools for people preparing for online skills tests in job applications, and for anyone who wants to get faster at everyday work skills.

The first tool is Excel formula practice. Every exercise uses a small, realistic sales table. You type a real formula, and the page calculates it the way Excel would. It checks the result, the function you used, and that the formula refers to cells instead of typed numbers.

The site is maintained by an independent developer. It is not affiliated with Microsoft or with any testing company.
