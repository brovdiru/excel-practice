About

Excel Formula Practice is a free trainer for people preparing for Excel tests in job interviews and for anyone who wants to write formulas faster.

Every exercise uses a small, realistic sales table. You type a real formula, and the page calculates it the way Excel would. It checks the result, the function you used, and that the formula refers to cells instead of typed numbers.

The site is maintained by an independent developer. It is not affiliated with Microsoft.
